Privacy Policy

Sacha Ristha – Matrimonial App

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tararam Solutions ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Sacha Ristha mobile application (package name com.tararamsolutions.sacharistha) and related services (the "App"). It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India, and the Google Play User Data policy.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of your data as described here.

Summary
  • Sacha Ristha is only for adults (18+) who are looking for a life partner.
  • We collect only what is needed to run your matrimonial profile, matches, chat, verification and payments.
  • Your mobile number, email and verification documents are never shown on your public profile.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time (see Account & data deletion).

1. Data we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address.
  • Profile details: gender, date of birth, height, marital status, religion, caste and sub-caste, mother tongue, education, occupation, employment type, current organization, income range, city/state/country, family details, "about me" and partner preferences.
  • Photos: profile photos you upload, and a selfie if you choose to verify your photos.
  • Communications: chat messages, interests sent and received, and reports you make about other users.
  • Payments & wallet: premium plan purchases, wallet balance and transactions, and referral history. Card, UPI and bank details are entered directly with our payment partner and are not stored by us.
  • Support: information you send us by email, such as reports and help requests.

1.2 Verification data (optional)

  • Aadhaar verification: your Aadhaar number is shared with an authorised verification provider only to send and confirm a one-time password (OTP). We store the verification result (for example, whether name, date of birth and gender matched) and the verification date.
  • Salary slip verification: the salary slip you upload is used to read your UAN, which is checked to confirm your employer.
  • GSTIN verification (business users): your GSTIN and the business details linked to it, and the registered mobile number used for OTP confirmation.
  • Verification documents are never shown to other users.

1.3 Data collected automatically

  • Device information (device model, operating system version), app version and platform.
  • Push notification token, so we can send you notifications.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic data used to keep the App working, improve it and prevent misuse.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, call logs or SMS messages.

3. On-device processing

Some checks run directly on your phone, and the images used for these checks are not sent to us for that purpose:

  • Face detection to make sure a profile photo or selfie contains a clear face.
  • Photo safety check to block inappropriate or explicit images before they are uploaded.
  • Text recognition to read details (such as UAN) from a salary slip you choose to upload.

4. How we use your data

  • To create, display and manage your matrimonial profile.
  • To suggest suitable matches based on your profile and partner preferences.
  • To enable interests, chat and notifications between users.
  • To verify identity, employment or business details when you request it.
  • To process premium plan payments, wallet transactions and referral rewards.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, fake profiles, abuse and misuse, and to act on reports.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other users can see your profile details and photos, subject to your settings and their plan.
  • Your mobile number, email, verification documents and verification selfie are never shown on your public profile.
  • Your biodata is shared with another person only when you choose to send it.
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked on sensitive screens to protect users' photos and chats.

7. Payments

Payments for premium plans and wallet top-ups are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy (razorpay.com/privacy). We receive only the payment status and transaction reference, not your full card, UPI or bank details.

8. Data security

Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Login tokens are kept in secure storage on your device. Access to personal data is limited to people who need it. No system is completely secure, but if a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Account & data deletion

You can delete your account in either of these ways:

  • In the App: open Account → Delete account and confirm.
  • By email: write to ankit.chauhan@tararamsolutions.com with the subject "Account deletion request" from your registered email, or include your registered mobile number.

After you request deletion, your profile is hidden from other users straight away. Your account is permanently deleted after a 7-day period, during which you can log in to cancel the request. After that, we delete or anonymise your profile, photos, chats, interests and verification results within 30 days.

We may keep some records for longer where the law requires it, for example payment and invoice records for tax purposes, or data needed to handle fraud, abuse or legal disputes.

10. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Once deleted, data is removed as described in section 9, except records we must keep by law, which are kept only for the period required.

11. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Get a summary of your personal data and how it is used.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data (most details can be edited in the App).
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. This may limit the features you can use.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer, and with the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

12. Age requirement

Sacha Ristha is only for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

13. Reporting users and safety

You can unmatch a user or report them from the chat menu. Reports are sent to our support team by email with the reported user's ID so we can review and take action, including removing the profile. Never share money, OTPs, passwords or bank details with anyone you meet on the App.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant, we will notify you in the App.
