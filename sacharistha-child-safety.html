Child Safety Standards – Sacha Ristha

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Sacha Ristha (package name com.tararamsolutions.sacharistha) is a matrimonial app operated by Tararam Solutions. It is only for adults aged 18 and above.

1. Age restriction

  • Users must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • We do not knowingly allow users under 18.
  • If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

We do not allow any content, conduct, or contact that involves the sexual abuse or exploitation of children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, solicitation of minors, and any sexual content involving anyone under 18.

We will remove such content, ban the account, and report to authorities where required by law.

3. How users can report concerns

  • In the app: open a chat → menu (three dots) → Report. This opens email to our support team with the reported user ID.
  • By email: write to ankit.chauhan@tararamsolutions.com with the subject "Child safety report" and include the user ID, screenshots if available, and details.

4. How we handle reports

  • We review child-safety and CSAE reports as a priority.
  • We may hide or remove profiles, ban accounts, and preserve evidence for legal requests.
  • Where required by Indian law, we report to the appropriate authorities.

5. Safety tools in the app

  • Report users from chat.
  • Unmatch a user to end the conversation.
  • Photo safety checks help block inappropriate images before upload.
  • Screenshots are blocked on chat and other sensitive screens.
